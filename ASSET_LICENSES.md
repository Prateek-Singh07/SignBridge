# Asset Licenses and Attribution

This document details the source, authority, licensing, and attribution for the Indian Sign Language (ISL) alphabet reference images bundled into SignBridge.

## Summary

- **Collection:** Official Indian Sign Language (ISL) Fingerspelling Alphabet Reference Materials
- **Authority / Publisher:** Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre (ISLRTC), Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan), Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India
- **Official Source Portals:**
  - ISLRTC Official Website: [https://islrtc.nic.in/](https://islrtc.nic.in/)
  - ISLRTC Awareness Materials: [https://islrtc.nic.in/document-category/awareness-material/](https://islrtc.nic.in/document-category/awareness-material/)
  - Official ISL Dictionary: [https://divyangjan.depwd.gov.in/islrtc/](https://divyangjan.depwd.gov.in/islrtc/)
- **Source Document:** "Alphabet in ISL" (Official ISLRTC Public Awareness Resource, PDF S3 WAAS Repository: `https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3f7e2b2b75b04175610e5a00c1e221ebb/uploads/2025/06/202506021220778202.pdf`)
- **Permitted Usage & Purpose:** Released by the Government of India for public awareness, educational accessibility, and community learning of Indian Sign Language.
- **Asset Processing:**
  - Individual letter illustrations extracted directly from the official ISLRTC awareness publication at ultra-high resolution (8× rasterization scale).
  - Background converted to clean transparent alpha channel preserving original stroke fidelity and anti-aliasing.
  - Centered on uniform square canvases with standard padding.
  - Exported into Xcode asset catalog sets (`isl_letter_a` through `isl_letter_z`) at standard iOS resolutions: `@1x` (200×200), `@2x` (400×400), and `@3x` (600×600).
- **Linguistic System:** Indian Sign Language fingerspelling primarily uses a two-handed alphabet system (derived from the BANZSL language family), in which the non-dominant hand frequently acts as a reference base while the dominant hand forms vowels and consonant articulations.
